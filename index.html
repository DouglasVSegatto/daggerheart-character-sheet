How to Use

▼
Tabs: Sheet for gameplay stats, Story for narrative/backstory, Support for links & help.
Saving: All changes auto-save to your browser. Use Export to download a backup JSON file. Use Import to restore from a file.
Cards & Abilities: Tap + Add to browse the SRD library. Click any card on your sheet to read it in full. Star up to 5 domain cards for your active loadout.
Gear: Two weapon slots and one armor slot are fixed. Use + Gear to add shields, rings, cloaks, etc.
Inventory: Track gold (handfuls/bags) and add/remove items with quantities.
Theme: Pick an accent color with the color swatch. Use the slide toggle for dark/light mode.
Reorder Sections: Hold and drag any section on the Sheet tab to rearrange the layout to your preference.
Tips: Export often — localStorage is tied to your browser. Clearing browser data will lose unsaved sheets.
